### Description

The ASPP Official Benchmark Evaluation Suite v1.1 is the formal evaluation framework for The Allie Scenario Prompting Protocol (ASPP). It extends the v1.0 preprint and specification with a benchmark specification, an evaluation rubric with stage-level scoring guidance, a structured test case (ASPP-SCENARIO-001: Structured Decision Optimization Evaluation), annotated response quality examples (passing, borderline, failing), machine-readable schema definitions (JSON and YAML), and a structured Zenodo metadata record. ASPP is a scenario-constrained, outcome-first prompt engineering methodology that guides AI systems to produce one best actionable output through the S.C.E.N.A.R.I.O. framework. Creator: Darren L. Allie, Director of AI Workflow Optimization and AI Business Improvement, Azure & Verdant Vistas, LLC.
