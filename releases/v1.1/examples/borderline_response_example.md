## Evaluator Notes

This response is borderline. All eight S.C.E.N.A.R.I.O. stage headings are present, but several stages are superficial. The recommendation is present but weakly supported. The action plan lacks specificity and success criteria. No fabricated data is present, but the analysis does not engage with the specific constraints provided in the scenario.

---

## Response Text

**S — State the Desired Outcome**

The goal is to reduce maintenance response time to under 24 hours.

---

**C — Clarify the Scenario Context**

The company manages residential units across multiple locations. They have a small maintenance team and no current software.

---

**E — Examine Constraints and Risks**

- Budget: $8,000 available
- Staffing is limited
- The 90-day timeline is tight

---

**N — Narrow the Decision Space**

The main options are software, more staff, or process changes. Since the budget is limited and hiring is not an option, the best approach is software or process changes.

---

**A — Analyze with Logic, Reasoning, Coding, or Calculation**

Software platforms are available at various price points. A reasonable option would be to find a platform that fits within the $8,000 budget. Process changes could also help, such as creating clearer intake procedures or communication protocols.

---

**R — Recommend the Best Single Output**

Implement a maintenance management software platform and update the scheduling process.

---

**I — Indicate Additional Outcomes Only When Necessary**

Alternatively, the company could also consider restructuring the part-time scheduler role into a full-time position if software alone is not sufficient.

---

**O — Output an Executable Action Plan**

1. Research software options
2. Select a platform
3. Train the team
4. Monitor response times
5. Adjust as needed

### Key Weaknesses

- **Context stage** omits material facts from the scenario (unit count, location count, technician count, scheduler role, current response time of 72 hours).
- **Analysis stage** does not engage with specific budget figures, capacity analysis, or timeline math.
- **Action plan** lacks concrete timelines, onboarding steps, and a defined success criterion.
- **Alternative** in the I stage is presented as a near-equal option rather than a conditional fallback, slightly diluting the primary recommendation.

### What Would Raise This Score

- Reference specific scenario facts in the C and A stages
- Provide a budget estimate or capacity calculation in the A stage
- Provide a specific timeline in the action plan
- Add a measurable success criterion at day 90
